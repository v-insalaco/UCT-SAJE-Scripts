Notes about pass marks 

 

  • If you've achieved 50% or more you've passed this assessment 
  • If you've achieved less than 40%, you've failed this assessment and will need to resit in the supplementary period 
  • To pass the module overall, you need a module mark of 50% or more 
  • If you fail the module (less than 50%) and achieved below 50% in this assessment, you'll need to resit this assessment in the supplementary period 

 

Notes about examiners and moderation 

 

Your assessments are marked by internal examiners, with marks ratified by external examiners (senior academics from other universities) to ensure that marks are appropriate.   

 

Assessments which contribute to the weighted mean mark, and which are weighted as 10% or more of a module mark, are internally moderated by academic staff.  Moderation refers to a range of processes which are conducted by academic staff to ensure that the marking of assessments is: 

 

  • Accurate 
  • Appropriate to the level of the assessment 
  • Comparable with equivalent assessments
